URA Q1 2026 Singapore Real Estate Market Summary
Source: https://www.ura.gov.sg/Corporate/Media-Room/Media-Releases/pr26-31

Private Residential Sector

The private residential market showed modest growth in the first quarter. Property prices increased by 0.9%, matching the average quarterly rise seen throughout 2025. Rental values grew marginally at 0.3%. Non-landed properties outperformed landed properties, with prices rising 1.3% compared to a 0.4% decline for landed properties. The outside central region led rental gains at 1.0% quarterly growth.

Transaction activity softened from the prior quarter, with developers selling 2,013 units versus 2,940 units previously. Resale transactions dominated, accounting for nearly 60% of all sales. The vacancy rate edged up to 6.2% from 6.0%, with regional variations ranging from 5.2% in outer zones to 8.2% in prime locations.

Looking ahead, approximately 55,800 residential units are expected to complete over coming years, with about 4,600 units from government land sales programs, 50% above the decade-long average for half-yearly releases.

Commercial Markets

Office space showed stability with a marginal 0.2% price appreciation, though rental values declined slightly by 0.2%. Island-wide office vacancy improved to 10.8% from 11.1%. The retail sector performed more robustly, with prices climbing 2.2% despite rental decreases of 0.6%. Retail vacancy remained flat at 6.3%.

Economic Outlook

Officials cautioned households to "exercise prudence when purchasing property and taking out mortgage loans," citing heightened macroeconomic uncertainty.
